> PRIVACY_POLICY

This Privacy Policy explains how The LightWorkers ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you play Sunarius: A Post-Apocalyptic RPG (the "Game") or contact us about it.

We collect only the information needed to run the Game, save progress, keep the Game fair and secure, and provide support. For privacy questions, contact support@sunarius.com.

> 1. INFORMATION_WE_COLLECT

When you use the Game and its online features, we may collect and store:

  • a unique account identifier and a protected device or installation identifier used to create or restore a guest account;
  • your e-mail address if you choose e-mail sign-in, or a protected identifier from Google if you choose Google sign-in. We do not receive or store your Google password;
  • character name, appearance, level, score, cloud saves, and other gameplay data needed to save and restore your progress;
  • technical information needed for the online service, such as app version, platform, operating-system version, device information, IP address, and connection or error logs;
  • gameplay, validation, and security records needed to prevent cheating, fraud, technical abuse, and other violations of the Game rules; and
  • information you choose to send us in a support request.

We do not ask for your real name, home address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone data, or payment-card details through the Game.

> 2. HOW_WE_USE_INFORMATION

We use this information to:

  • create and secure accounts, and allow players to sign in;
  • provide cloud saves and restore characters and progress;
  • operate online features, including leaderboards where available;
  • answer support requests, fix problems, and improve Game stability and security; and
  • detect, investigate, and prevent cheating, fraud, exploits, abuse, and violations of the Game rules.

> 3. FAIR_PLAY_AND_BANS

To protect the Game and its players, we may review relevant account, gameplay, and security information when investigating a suspected violation. We may remove a player from a leaderboard, limit access to a feature, or permanently ban an account for serious or repeated violations.

This includes cheating; using unauthorized software; exploiting bugs; manipulating saves, scores, rewards, or game currency; fraud; account trading; ban evasion; harassment, threats, hateful or unlawful conduct; or other misuse of the Game. Where necessary to prevent ban evasion, a permanent ban may also apply to protected account or device identifiers associated with the banned account.

To appeal a ban, write to support@sunarius.com and include the case ID shown in the Game, if available.

> 4. INFORMATION_VISIBLE_TO_OTHER_PLAYERS

If you participate in a leaderboard or other public Game feature, your character name, level, score, rank, and selected character appearance may be visible to other players. Please do not use your real name, contact information, or other personal information as your character name.

> 5. THIRD_PARTY_SERVICES

We use trusted providers where needed to operate the Game, such as server hosting, database storage, transactional e-mail, technical security, and Google sign-in.

We may also disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the Game, our players, our rights, or the public from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

> 6. STORAGE_AND_ACCOUNT_DELETION

We keep account and cloud-save data while your account is active. We keep technical and security records only for as long as reasonably necessary to run, secure, and improve the Game, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

You can request deletion of your account from within the Game. Deleting an account removes the account, characters, cloud saves, and associated sign-in data that we are not required to keep. We may retain limited information where necessary to comply with law, resolve a dispute, prevent fraud, or enforce a ban.

Deleting the Game from your device or logging out does not delete your account. Deleting a character removes that character's cloud-save data but does not delete the whole account.

> 7. YOUR_RIGHTS_SECURITY_AND_CONTACT

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain processing. To make a request, e-mail support@sunarius.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We may ask for reasonable information to confirm that the request relates to your account.

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No online service is completely secure, so please keep your device and sign-in methods secure.

The Game is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use online services in their country without parental consent. If you believe that a child has provided us personal information without required consent, contact us at support@sunarius.com.

We may update this Policy when the Game or our data practices change. We will post the updated version on our website and change the "Last updated" date.
