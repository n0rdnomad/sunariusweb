Best Games Like Battle Brothers on Android

Published October 17, 2026 · By the developer of Sunarius

1. Sunarius — Battle Brothers Meets the Post-Apocalypse

  • Best for: Players who want squad management, hex-grid combat and a dangerous world to explore on Android.
  • Platforms: Android
  • Price: Free to download, with ads and optional in-app purchases. Ads can be removed.

Sunarius takes one of Battle Brothers' most appealing ideas — leading a group of fighters rather than a single overpowered hero — and drops it into a post-apocalyptic wasteland.

The premise is familiar, but the setting changes the flavour of the adventure considerably. Instead of medieval mercenaries chasing contracts across a war-torn countryside, you're assembling a squad in a world of raiders, patrols, caravans and competing factions. Survival depends on more than winning fights. You need supplies, equipment and a way to get your people from one dangerous place to another.

Combat takes place on a hex grid, where positioning and squad composition can make the difference between a clean victory and an expensive disaster. Characters develop through classes, levels, traits and perks, gradually becoming specialists rather than interchangeable bodies carrying weapons. As your squad grows, the people you recruit begin to define how you approach encounters.

The world map adds another layer to the experience. Dynamically generated groups travel across the wasteland, giving exploration a sense of movement beyond simply clicking through a list of locations. You can travel using trikes, jeeps and caravans, while food and water add a practical consideration to your journeys.

There's also a broader RPG here. Sunarius features more than 100 quests with multiple endings, dialogue, and factions that respond to your actions. That gives it a different rhythm from Battle Brothers, where the appeal often comes from the emergent stories produced by your company's successes and failures.

The distinction matters. Sunarius isn't simply trying to recreate Battle Brothers with different graphics. It combines squad tactics with a post-apocalyptic setting, quest-driven exploration and survival mechanics. Players who enjoy building a capable team and navigating a hostile world should find plenty to interest them.

The verdict: Sunarius is a strong option if you want a squad-based tactical RPG on Android rather than another mobile game built around disposable units and automatic battles. Its hex-grid combat and character progression will appeal to fans of Battle Brothers, while the wasteland setting and quest system give the experience its own identity.

2. Wartales

Wartales, from Shiro Games, is the most direct Battle Brothers alternative on this list. You lead a band of mercenaries in a harsh medieval world where, in the developers' own words, combat, death and a thirst for riches dictate daily life. You chase profit across an open world and develop your camp to help the band recover. Each companion has a specialisation, skills, weapon preferences and a personality, and you shape them through progression and crafting. Battles are turn-based and reward picking the right mix of characters, equipment and tactics. Wartales left early access in 2023 and supports online co-op for up to four players, which Battle Brothers does not offer.

  • Genre: Open-world tactical RPG
  • Platforms: PC (Windows), Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One
  • Price: Paid
  • Release year: 2023 (early access from 2021)
  • Why it is similar: a mercenary company in an open medieval world, turn-based battles, companions you build up
  • How it differs: more exploration, professions and crafting, plus online co-op
  • Official page: Wartales on Steam

3. Jagged Alliance 3

Jagged Alliance 3, developed by Haemimont Games and published by THQ Nordic in 2023, is the modern take on hiring mercenaries for a dangerous contract. In the country of Grand Chien the elected president has gone missing and a paramilitary force called the Legion has taken the countryside, so his family hires you. You pick from a large cast of mercenaries with their own personalities, quirks and backstories, then control territory, train the locals, command several squads and earn the money to keep going. Fights are turn-based, with plenty of loot and weapons to salvage and customise, and the campaign can also be played in online co-op.

  • Genre: Turn-based tactics with a strategic layer
  • Platforms: PC (Windows), PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One
  • Price: Paid
  • Release year: 2023
  • Why it is similar: managing a mercenary roster, turn-based battles, a strategic layer with territory to hold
  • How it differs: a modern military setting with firearms and a cast of hand-written characters
  • Official page: Jagged Alliance 3 on Steam

4. The Banner Saga

The Banner Saga, the 2014 debut from Stoic, trades the sandbox for a story. A Viking-inspired caravan crosses a dying world, and building and managing that caravan is, as the store page puts it, critical to the survival of an entire civilisation. You decide who fights and what to do when trouble arrives on the road, and battles are turn-based tactical fights with hand-drawn art. Losses are real: whether a character is lost for good depends on who you take into battle and what you decide afterwards. The game also came to mobile: it was released on Android in 2014, as well as on iOS, and reached Nintendo Switch in 2018.

  • Genre: Story-driven tactical RPG
  • Platforms: PC (Windows, macOS), Nintendo Switch, iOS, Android
  • Price: Paid
  • Release year: 2014
  • Why it is similar: a group you must keep alive, a caravan to manage, turn-based battles, permanent losses
  • How it differs: a linear, hand-drawn story campaign instead of an open sandbox
  • Official page: The Banner Saga on Steam

5. Into the Breach

Into the Breach, by Subset Games, is the smallest and sharpest game here. You command a squad of mechs on small maps and defend cities from the Vek, giant creatures breeding beneath the earth. Every enemy attack is shown in advance, so each turn is a puzzle about positioning, and every run is a fresh, randomly generated campaign in which you find new weapons and pilots. It was released on PC in 2018 and on Nintendo Switch the same year. On Android and iOS it is available through Netflix: the mobile Advanced Edition launched in 2022 for Netflix subscribers, with a touch interface and no ads or in-app purchases.

  • Genre: Turn-based tactics, roguelite
  • Platforms: PC (Windows, macOS, Linux), Nintendo Switch, Android and iOS (with a Netflix subscription)
  • Price: Paid on PC and Switch; included with a Netflix subscription on mobile
  • Release year: 2018
  • Why it is similar: a small squad, turn-based battles where positioning is everything
  • How it differs: no roster economy or open world; short, puzzle-like runs instead of a long campaign
  • Official page: Into the Breach on Steam

FAQ: games like Battle Brothers on mobile

Can I play Battle Brothers on mobile?

There is no official Android or iOS version of Battle Brothers. The game is available on PC, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation and Xbox.

What is the closest game to Battle Brothers on Android?

For squad management and hex-grid tactics on Android, try Sunarius, our own early-access squad RPG. If you have a Netflix subscription, Into the Breach is an excellent, if much smaller, tactics game on mobile.

Is Wartales like Battle Brothers?

Yes, it is the closest match on PC: you run a mercenary band in an open medieval world and fight turn-based battles. It puts more weight on exploration and crafting, and it adds online co-op for up to four players.

Which of these games can I play in co-op?

Wartales supports online co-op for up to four players, and Jagged Alliance 3 has an online co-op campaign. Sunarius, The Banner Saga and Into the Breach are single-player.

Conclusion

No game replaces Battle Brothers exactly, but each of these keeps a piece of it: Wartales the mercenary sandbox, Jagged Alliance 3 the roster and contracts, The Banner Saga the cost of keeping people alive, and Into the Breach the purity of turn-based positioning. If you want a squad to lead on your phone, try Sunarius, our hex-grid squad tactics game for Android, and tell us what a Battle Brothers fan would want next.
